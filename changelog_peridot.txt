3.0-peridot-20260906-1558-OFFICIAL
==================================

Camera
  * MiuiCamera is gone. It was the single largest item in the ROM (a 187 MB
    APK plus helper libraries) and it had a real bug: after a shot, its
    post-processing was killed once you left the app, so the photo stayed
    as a hidden pending MediaStore row and only appeared in the gallery at
    the NEXT camera launch, written from the thumbnail at low quality.
    Aperture (the stock LineageOS camera) is the camera app again; Glimpse
    remains the gallery. Xiaomi's HDR/night/portrait processing goes with
    MiuiCamera; Aperture uses the vendor camera HAL directly.
  * Aperture is set up exactly as LineageOS 23.2 does for peridot: the
    ultrawide is exposed as a separate lens next to the main camera and the
    front camera, and 60 fps video is available on both (up to 4K on the
    back). A second, redundant 1x back-lens button may show up because the
    HAL also exposes the main sensor standalone; hiding it needs an
    on-device measurement and will follow.

Sound & vibration
  * Ships silent. Off by default: dial pad tones, touch sounds, screen
    locking sound, charging sound, charging vibration, touch feedback, and
    the ring, notification, alarm and media vibration intensities (the
    sliders stay, so any of it can be turned back on). Screenshot sound is
    off by default too.
  * The on/off toggles are first-boot seeds: they apply on a clean flash;
    a dirty flash keeps the values you already have. The vibration
    intensities and the screenshot sound apply on a dirty flash as well,
    unless you had already moved that slider or switch.
  * Accessibility haptics and the hardware haptics (power button,
    fingerprint) are untouched.

Freezer
  * The camera and gallery apps can no longer be frozen, so "open gallery
    from the camera" and "take a photo" from other apps keep working.
